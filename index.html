About ANSHÉA

ANSHÉA began with a simple belief: a signature scent should be found, not chosen in a hurry at a counter.

Each of our six Eau de Parfums is composed around a single feeling, from the bright spark of Éclat Noir to the slow warmth of Cuir Velours. We concentrate them as Eau de Parfum so they stay close to the skin from morning into evening.

That is why the Discovery Set sits at the centre of the house. We would rather you live with all six for a week and come back for the one you love.
